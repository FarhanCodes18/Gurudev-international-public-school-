Future of Learning

Robotics & STEM Innovation

Our state-of-the-art Robotics Lab is among the most advanced in the region, equipped with Arduino microcontrollers, LEGO Mindstorms EV3, Raspberry Pi, 3D FDM & SLA printers, laser cutters, and complete IoT development stations.

Students from Class VI learn robotics, programming, electronics, and design thinking. Our teams have won multiple national and state-level competitions including Gold at the National Robotics Championship 2026.

STEM Curriculum

Programming

Scratch, Python, C++, and Blockly coding from Class VI. Students build real working programs and games.

Circuits, sensors, actuators, motor control, and PCB design for hands-on electrical engineering experience.

Introduction to artificial intelligence concepts, image recognition, and neural networks for senior students.
