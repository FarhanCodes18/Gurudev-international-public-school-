State-of-the-Art Computer Lab

Our computer lab features 60 high-performance workstations with Intel i7 processors, 16GB RAM, 24-inch HD monitors, and 1Gbps optical fibre internet. Students learn Python, Java, Scratch, web development, and app building.

The lab is fitted with an interactive whiteboard, HD projector, and multimedia headsets at every station. All content is filtered and child-safe.
