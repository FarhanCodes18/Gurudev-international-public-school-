How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to become part of the Gurudev International Public School family. We welcome passionate educators and dedicated professionals.

1

Download Form

Download the official Application Form PDF and fill it carefully with your complete details.

2

Attach Documents

Collect required documents — resume, degree certificates, experience letters, and photo ID.

3

Submit Application

Submit your filled form + documents via email at gips.balaghat@gmail.com or in person at the school office.

4

Interview & Selection

Shortlisted candidates will be called for a demo class + personal interview with the Principal.

Required Documents

  • Completely filled Application Form
  • Updated Resume / CV
  • Educational Qualifications (B.Ed / M.Ed / Degree)
  • Experience Certificate (if applicable)
  • Government Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport)
  • 2 Recent Passport-size Photographs
  • TET/CTET Certificate (for teaching positions)
