FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the eligibility criteria for admission to Class I?

For admission to Class I, the child must be at least 6 years of age as of 31st March of the academic year. Admission is granted based on age verification and availability of seats.

Is Gurudev International Public School affiliated with CBSE?

Yes, Gurudev International Public School is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi. Affiliation No: 1030822.

Does the school provide transport facilities?

Yes, we provide safe and reliable transport services covering 30+ routes across the city. All buses are GPS-tracked, equipped with CCTVs, and have a lady attendant for safety.

What is the fee structure for the academic year 2026–27?

The fee structure varies by class and stream. Please download our Fee Structure document from the Admission page or contact our admission office at 7770822000, 9753187666 for detailed information.

Are scholarships available for meritorious students?

Yes! We offer merit-based scholarships of up to 50% fee waiver for students who score above 95% in the previous class. We also have scholarships for sports achievements and financial assistance for economically weaker sections.

How can I access the Student Portal?

Students can access the portal at student-portal.html using their roll number and password provided during admission. For login issues, contact the school IT department.

What is the student-teacher ratio?

We maintain an optimal student-teacher ratio of 1:25. This ensures that every child receives personalised attention and guidance from our expert educators.

What extracurricular activities are offered?

We believe in holistic development and offer a wide range of activities including Robotics, Coding, Classical & Western Dance, Music, Art & Craft, and various Sports (Football, Cricket, Basketball, etc.).

How does the school ensure student safety?

Student safety is our top priority. The campus is secured with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, restricted entry, trained security personnel, and GPS-enabled transport with female attendants.
