Hands-On Science

World-Class Science Laboratories

Gurudev International boasts three separate, fully equipped science laboratories — Physics, Chemistry, and Biology — each designed to provide students with genuine hands-on experimental experience aligned with the CBSE curriculum.

All labs are equipped with modern instruments, safety equipment, and waste disposal systems, ensuring a safe, productive learning environment for all students from Class VI onwards.
