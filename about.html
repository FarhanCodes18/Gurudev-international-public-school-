Our Story

Excellence Since 2005

Gurudev International Public School was founded in 2005 with a singular vision — to create an educational institution that nurtures not just academic excellence, but the complete development of every child.

Over two decades, we have grown from a humble beginning to a prestigious institution trusted by over 5,000 families. Our CBSE-affiliated curriculum, combined with world-class facilities and a passionate team of educators, creates an environment where every child can thrive.

Today, Gurudev International stands as a beacon of educational excellence — recognised nationally for our academic results, sports achievements, and innovative STEM programs.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating, inclusive learning environment that empowers every student to achieve their highest potential — academically, socially, and emotionally.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognised institution that produces confident, compassionate, and capable leaders who contribute meaningfully to society and the world.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Compassion, Innovation, Collaboration, and Respect for all — the six pillars that guide every decision at Gurudev International.

Our Journey

School History

2005
Foundation Stone Laid
Gurudev International Public School was established with just 200 students and a vision to transform education in the region.
2008
CBSE Affiliation Achieved
Received full CBSE affiliation and launched Classes IX–X, marking a major milestone in our academic journey.
2012
Senior Secondary Added
Expanded to Class XII with Science, Commerce, and Arts streams. First batch produced district toppers.
2016
New Campus Inaugurated
State-of-the-art 10-acre campus inaugurated with smart classrooms, science labs, and a 800-seat auditorium.
2020
Robotics Lab & STEM Centre
Launched one of the region's most advanced robotics and STEM labs. Students participated in national competitions within months.
2025
20 Years of Excellence
Celebrated two decades of transformative education with 5000+ students and 150+ awards. Recognised as a Top School by national education bodies.
