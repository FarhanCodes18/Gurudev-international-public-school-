A Message from Our Chairman

Education is the Light of the Soul

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. At Gurudev International, we wield this weapon with wisdom, compassion, and unwavering commitment."

Dear Parents, Students, and Well-Wishers,

It is with immense pride and deep gratitude that I welcome you to Gurudev International Public School. When we laid the foundation of this institution in 2005, we had a dream — a dream to create a school that would not merely produce academically proficient students, but would shape human beings of character, integrity, and global vision.

Two decades later, that dream has blossomed into a living reality. With over 5,000 students, 200+ dedicated educators, and a track record of academic excellence that speaks for itself, Gurudev International stands tall as one of the premier educational institutions of the region.

But numbers, however impressive, only tell part of the story. What truly defines us is the transformation we witness in our students — the shy child who becomes a confident speaker, the struggling student who becomes a topper, the dreamer who becomes an innovator. These transformations are our greatest achievement.

At Gurudev International, we believe education is not a destination — it is a journey. A journey that shapes values, builds character, and ignites the passion to learn, serve, and lead. We are committed to walking alongside every student on this extraordinary journey.

I invite you to be a part of our growing family. Together, let us build a brighter future for the next generation.

Sh. Rajendra Gupta

Chairman, Gurudev International Public School
