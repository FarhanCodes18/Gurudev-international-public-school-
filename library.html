Our Digital Library

The Gurudev International Library houses over 20,000 physical books, 5,000+ digital e-books, and subscriptions to leading academic journals and periodicals. Open to students from Classes I-XII, the library fosters a love of reading and independent research.

Our computerised OPAC system lets students search and reserve books online. The library features reading zones, group study rooms, and digital learning stations.
